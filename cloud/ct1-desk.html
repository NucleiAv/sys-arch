Review the tier 1 claim

A team says its payment API is tier 1 ready. Review the design.

Round 1. Find the gapsRound 2. Fix it

The API runs in two regions. Fraud scoring and the KMS keys exist only in Mumbai. Auto scaling in Hyderabad starts from 10 percent of peak. Failover is automatic on any failed health check. Nobody has tested it.

Round 1. Find the gaps

Round 2. Fix it

Pocket card. Keep this

  • Two active regions, each sized for full peak.
  • Any two AZs carry the region. Static stability.
  • Cells. Home region per customer. Global tables.
  • Idempotency keys and reconciliation.
  • Routing control failover, tested every quarter.
  • Tokens, HSM, mTLS, WAF, no internet ingress.
