Pocket card. Keep this

  • Tier by business impact. Numbers are company defined.
  • Tier 0 foundation must be the most available.
  • Tier 1 two active regions, cells, static stability.
  • Tier 2 multi AZ and warm standby. Tier 3 backups to another region.
  • Tier 4 Spot and re runnable jobs.
  • Every tier protects data by its class, not its tier.
