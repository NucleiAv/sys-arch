Fix the swarm

Your peer to peer update system for a game launcher has problems. Find them and fix them.

Round 1. Spot the issueRound 2. Benefits and workarounds

The launcher shares 30 GB game updates between players to save bandwidth. Players report slow starts, some corrupt installs, and a security researcher says updates could be faked.

Round 1. Spot the issue

Round 2. Benefits and workarounds

Pocket card. Keep this

  • Name data by its hash. Check every piece.
  • DHT with XOR distance. About log n steps.
  • Rarest first. Tit for tat. Optimistic unchoke.
  • Hashes protect data, not discovery. Sign the source.
