Find the weak spots

A competitor's platform design leaked online. Review it as a security architect.

Round 1. Identify the issuesRound 2. Fix and justify

Their design. Devices share one TLS client certificate. The cloud can open connections to devices through a VPN to each hospital. Updates are signed by a key stored on the build server. Patient names are sent to the cloud for support. Debug UART pins are active on the board.

Round 1. Identify the issues

Round 2. Fix and justify

Pocket card. Keep this

  • Small scale, big trust problems. Lead with boundaries.
  • Unique device identity. Mutual TLS.
  • Outbound only. Segment devices.
  • Signed, approved, staged, reversible updates.
  • Safety never depends on security. Evidence by design.
