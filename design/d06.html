Design a video streaming platform

Creators upload videos, and millions watch them on phones and TVs without buffering, even on slow networks. Like YouTube or Netflix.

The ask

Users upload videos. The platform prepares them for every screen and network speed, then streams them to viewers around the world. The video must start fast and keep playing smoothly when the network gets worse.

Pin down the requirements

What it must do

  • Upload videos up to a few GB.
  • Watch on phone, web and TV, with quality that adapts to the network.
  • Video page with title, views and thumbnails.

How well it must do it

  • Playback starts in under 2 seconds.
  • Little or no buffering.
  • Uploads are never lost, processing can take minutes.
  • Massive read bandwidth worldwide.

Out of scope, say it out loud

  • Comments, recommendations, live streaming, payments for creators.

Do the math

1 million uploads a day, average 10 minutes. 1 billion views a day, average 5 minutes watched at about 3 Mbps.

Key point

The data volume is in the petabytes and the bandwidth in terabits. Storage is object storage, delivery is a CDN, and your servers mostly handle metadata.

The API

POST /v1/videos                -> { video_id, upload_url }   presigned, resumable
POST /v1/videos/{id}/complete  -> starts processing
GET  /v1/videos/{id}           -> title, status, thumbnails, manifest_url
GET  {cdn}/videos/{id}/master.m3u8   -> list of quality levels
GET  {cdn}/videos/{id}/720p/seg_0042.ts  -> one 4 second piece

The big picture

  1. Upload service hands out resumable presigned URLs and records video metadata.
  2. Processing pipeline with a queue, chunked parallel transcoding, thumbnail and safety checks. It is a workflow, so each step retries on its own.
  3. Storage in object storage for raw and processed files, with older and rarely watched videos moved to cheaper tiers.
  4. Delivery through a CDN using HLS or DASH segments and manifests.
  5. Metadata and views in a database with a cache, view counts aggregated in batches.

Deep dives

Adaptive bitrate

Each video exists in several qualities, each cut into 2 to 6 second segments. The player picks the quality for each next segment based on measured speed and buffer level. Formats are HLS and MPEG-DASH.

Cost control

Most views go to a small share of videos. Transcode new videos to a few qualities at first, add more if they get popular, and push rarely watched videos to cheaper storage.

Start time

Start at a low quality to begin fast, prefetch the first segments when a user hovers, and keep the manifest cached at the edge.

View counts

Do not write to the database on every view. Send view events to a stream and add them up every few seconds, see lesson 5.2.

Popular videos

A new viral video gets requested from every edge at once. Use a mid tier cache, sometimes called origin shield, so edges fetch from it instead of all hitting origin storage.

Security and abuse

Video platforms deal with piracy and harmful content.

  • Use signed, expiring URLs for video segments, and DRM for paid content.
  • Scan uploads for malware and for known harmful content.
  • Limit upload size and rate, and process uploads in sandboxed workers.
