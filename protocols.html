How to use this page

  1. Choose a protocol from the list. On a phone, press All protocols.
  2. Press Play, or use Next and Back. The arrow keys also move between steps.
  3. Switch between Network, which shows packets moving between machines, and Sequence, which shows every message in time order.
  4. Read the Current step panel. Click a packet in the Packet inspector to see its fields.
  5. Open Practice and answer the questions.

What the layer numbers mean

The badges such as L7 refer to the OSI model, a seven layer way to describe networks. L2 is the local link, such as Ethernet and Wi-Fi. L3 is IP addressing and routing. L4 is transport, such as TCP and UDP. L5 to L7 are sessions, encryption and applications. Real protocols do not always fit one layer exactly, so the badge shows where each one mostly works.

In plain words

Addresses in the animations use documentation ranges, such as 192.0.2.0/24, 198.51.100.0/24 and 203.0.113.0/24, and private ranges such as 192.168.1.0/24. They are examples, not real servers.
