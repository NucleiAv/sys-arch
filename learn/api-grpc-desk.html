Design the contract for a delivery tracker

Two backend teams need a service contract for live delivery tracking. Choose the call types and protect the contract.

Round 1. Pick call typesRound 2. Write and protect

Parcelly has a dispatch service and a tracking service. Dispatch asks tracking for a parcel's location. The customer app wants live updates. Drivers' phones send a GPS point every 5 seconds.
