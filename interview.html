Interview tip

Interviewers care less about the "right" design and more about how you get there. Ask questions first, say your numbers out loud, draw simple boxes, and name the trade-off every time you make a choice.

The answer method

Use the same six steps for every design question. Spend your 45 minutes roughly like this.

StepWhat you doTime
1. RequirementsAsk what it must do and how well. Agree on what is out of scope.5 min
2. EstimatesUsers, requests per second, storage per year. Round hard.5 min
3. APIThe few calls the client makes.5 min
4. Data modelThe main tables or records and how you look them up.5 min
5. High level designBoxes and arrows for the main flows.10 min
6. Deep divesScale the hottest part, handle failure, name trade-offs.15 min

The full method with an example is in design 00.
